How it works

  1. 01

    Set your Signals

    Up to six cards: time, pulse, weather, calendar, or custom.

  2. 02

    Type anywhere

    Switch to the ContextKey keyboard in any app.

  3. 03

    Tap INJECT+

    Your selected context lands at the cursor.

Features

6 Signal cards

Customizable live context: time, Apple Health pulse, weather, calendar, and custom text.

INJECT+

Choose exactly which cards go in. Nothing is inserted until you tap.

DICTATE NOTE

Add an optional voice note to any card, inserted as text.

Apple Watch

Companion app syncs directly with your iPhone.

Private by design. No ContextKey servers, no account, no tracking. The keyboard never logs what you type. Privacy policy →
